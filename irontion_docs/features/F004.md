# F004: Layout and Navigation (Sidebar, Responsive)

How the app shell adapts to the window, and how the sidebar folds in and out.

> [!info] Overview
> - The sidebar can be **folded away and brought back** (button, or `Ctrl+B`)
> - The layout **adapts to the window width**: sidebar, drawer or tab bar
> - The theme switch is **not** in the sidebar. It lives in Settings only ([[F003]])

## Sidebar

| Window width | Sidebar |
| ------------ | ------- |
| 1024px and up | Inline on the left. Folding it away gives the space to the content |
| 640 to 1023px | A **drawer** that slides over the content with a backdrop. Starts closed; it closes after you pick a page or click the backdrop |
| Under 640px | No sidebar. A **tab bar** at the bottom with five tabs: Today, Blocks, Calendar, Notes and **More** (Activities, Insights, Reminders, Settings). See Decisions |

- The sidebar and the drawer list all eight pages: Today, Blocks, Activities, Insights, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, with Settings at the bottom
- **Fold in:** the button at the top of the sidebar, or `Ctrl+B` (`Cmd+B` on macOS)
- **Fold out:** the button at the top left of the page, or `Ctrl+B`
- On wide windows the choice is **remembered** between launches. The drawer is temporary
- A folded sidebar is inert, so keyboard focus skips it
- The minimum window size is 420 × 520, so the app can sit in a narrow tile

## Responsive Rules

| Area | Wide | Narrow |
| ---- | ---- | ------ |
| Today | 3 stat tiles in a row, two charts side by side from 1024px | Stacked |
| Blocks | Grid + options panel side by side from 896px | Stacked; options become a compact bottom sheet ([[F001]]) |
| Activities | Tree + tags panel side by side from 896px | Tags below the tree |
| Insights | Chart and table side by side from 1024px | Stacked |
| Settings | Three theme cards in a row | One column |
| Notes ([[F006]]) | A board of cards as tall as their text, in columns at least 15rem wide, with the **tag panel in a column on the right** from 896px. The paper editor is its own page (`/notes/new`, `/notes/<id>`), at most 42rem wide | The tag panel moves **above** the board as one swipeable line; one column of cards; the paper fills the width |
| Reminders ([[F008]]) | Cards in a grid, Upcoming and Past | One column |
| Calendar ([[F007]]) | Month grid and a day panel side by side from 896px; the paper editor opens in the panel | The day's list opens as a bottom sheet; the paper editor takes over the page (never a sheet) |
| Dialogs | 28rem (confirmations, tag edit), 32rem (most), 46rem (templates) | Never wider or taller than the window |
| Settings: Danger zone | The Advanced button sits beside the title; the Delete data row is one line | The row stacks and **Delete data…** takes the full width. (The delete dialog stacks its From and To pickers at every width) |

- Row actions that appear on hover (edit, archive, add) are always visible on touch screens, which have no hover
- No page may scroll sideways at any width (checked at 1280, 1024, 800 and 390px)

## Behavior

- [x] Fold the sidebar in and out with a button and `Ctrl+B`
- [x] Remember the choice on wide windows
- [x] Drawer on medium windows, tab bar on small ones
- [x] Theme switch removed from the sidebar (Settings only)
- [x] Notes and Calendar in the sidebar, the drawer and the tab bar (with **More**)
- [x] Every page works from 390px to 1280px wide
- [ ] Remember the last page and scroll position
- [ ] Collapse to an icon rail instead of hiding completely (if wanted)

## Decisions

| Decision | Why |
| -------- | --- |
| The phone tab bar keeps **five** tabs and adds a **More** tab instead of seven tabs | Seven tabs are about 55px each at 390px, too tight for labels in Thai or Korean. Five stay readable and touchable |
| Tabs: Today, Blocks, Calendar, Notes; More: Activities, Insights, Reminders, Settings | Today, the grid, notes and the calendar are daily use; activities and insights are set up or read less often, and Settings rarely |
| **More** opens a small menu above the bar. It closes on choosing a page, on `Esc` and on tapping away. It is highlighted while one of its pages is open | A menu keeps the bar fixed and needs no extra page |
| One list of pages in `lib/nav.ts` | The sidebar, the drawer and the tab bar cannot drift apart |

## Implementation

- `apps/desktop/src/lib/layout.svelte.ts`: sidebar state and window width
- `apps/desktop/src/lib/nav.ts`: the pages, which are tabs and which are under More, and `isActive` (Notes stays highlighted on `/notes/<id>`)
- `apps/desktop/src/lib/components/Sidebar.svelte`: sidebar and drawer
- `apps/desktop/src/lib/components/TabBar.svelte`: the phone tab bar and the More menu
- `apps/desktop/src/routes/+layout.svelte`: app shell and the way back when folded away
